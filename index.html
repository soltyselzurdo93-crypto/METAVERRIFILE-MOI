FAQ

We've included some frequently asked questions below. For more, visit our Help Centre on Facebook or Instagram.

How do I know if I'm eligible?

Meta Verified is available in selected regions for creators who meet certain eligibility requirements (requirements for Facebook and Instagram).

I already have a verified badge. What happens to it?

As we test and learn, there will be no changes to accounts on Facebook or Instagram that are already verified based on prior requirements. Existing verified badge holders can also apply for a Meta Verified subscription if they meet the eligibility requirements. Learn more about eligibility requirements on Facebook and Instagram.

What is the verification process?

You may be asked to provide a government-issued ID and, in selected regions, may be asked to submit a selfie video so that we can confirm your identity before being approved for a Meta Verified subscription. The ID may be required to match the profile name and photo of the Facebook or Instagram account that you're applying for. Once your profile has been verified, you may not be able to change your username or date of birth on your profile without going through the Meta Verified subscription and verification application process again. Learn more about the verification process on Facebook and Instagram.

As a creator, can I use one Meta Verified subscription on both Facebook and Instagram?

Yes. If you have your Facebook and Instagram accounts linked in the Accounts Centre, you may be eligible to subscribe to Meta Verified for two profiles for a discounted price.

What is the price to subscribe to Meta Verified for creators?

Meta Verified subscriptions are generally available in most regions for USD 11.99 when you subscribe from the web and USD 14.99 when you subscribe within the app for the Standard plan. Other plans are priced at USD 49.99/month for Plus, USD 49.99/month for Premium, USD 499.99/month for Max. You can begin the Meta Verified onboarding experience on Facebook or Instagram to see local pricing for your market.

*Pricing and plans may vary and may not be available if Meta Verified has not yet been rolled out in your region.

What if I'm interested in Meta Verified for businesses?

You can learn more and sign up on the Meta Verified for businesses website.

Do my Meta Verified benefits extend to my other profiles and accounts?

Your Meta Verified benefits (including support access) only apply to your subscribed profile and will not be available for profiles that are not Meta Verified.

After I've signed up for Meta Verified, can I change my plan selection?

Yes. You can change your plan selection at any time on the management screen for your Meta Verified subscription.

How do I change my plan selection?

To change your plan selection, you can go to the management screen for your Meta Verified subscription on your purchasing platform and device to select Explore other plans. Note: To upgrade to the Plus, Premium and Max plans, you will need to use your Instagram or Facebook apps on your mobile device. Features, availability and pricing may vary by region and app.

I have multiple accounts subscribed to Meta Verified. Can I upgrade/downgrade only for one of them?

If you have a bundled Meta Verified subscription covering both your Instagram and Facebook accounts, any upgrade or downgrade will apply to both accounts together. To choose different plans for Facebook and Instagram, you can purchase separate subscriptions for each account, allowing you to customise your plan selection individually.
